# Registering Data in FEDER catalog

The FEDER Catalog is a metadata registry that allows organizations to share their digal assets (datasets, models, data services) with the community.

The FEDER Data Platform provides a logically centralized, FAIR-aligned view of assets while the underlying data remains distributed and accessible through registered URLs. Assets already stored in an accessible location should remain in place and be registered in FEDER using their access URLs. For the proof of concept, FIRE Collaboratives requiring accessible storage may use the [FEDER Pelican/OSDF Origin](../../technical-tutorials/pelican-101.md); additional storage requirements can be discussed separately. The FEDER Catalog is a metadata catalog, not a data repository.

### Before Registering your Dataset

- Prepare your asset’s metadata. For guidance on each field, including which are required or optional, refer to the [Metadata Schema](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hPWpnMhSymKO1Po5n-aeic7D1wnRiF8n8RmzvgzAfdM/edit?gid=0#gid=0).

- Prepare each of your resources. A digital asset may contain multiple files or resources, such as documentation, schema files, data samples, and API endpoints. Ensure that each resource associated with the asset has a valid, updated access point.

## Digital Asset Registration Process

1 - Go to your Dashboard. In the left panel, click on *Catalog*, then select *Add to Catalog*.

2 - Click *Register Catalog Entry*.

<img src="../images/registration.png" style="border: 2px solid black;">

3a - **If you're registering an asset for the first time:** Click the button *New? Start here -->*. You will be prompted with step-by-step instructions on how to properly fill out your asset entry according to your case.

<img src="../images/start-here.png" style="border: 2px solid black;">

3b - **Not your first time registering an asset:** Click the *Quick Start* section. This will open a form with the primary fields ready to be filled out quickly. After filling out the mandatory fields, click *Quick Start* button at the bottom of the form.

<img src="../images/quick-start.png" style="border: 2px solid black;">

4 - To add individual files or resources, click *Start* in the *Items* followed by *Click to add Asset*. Fill out the form along with the the URLs or endpoints where your data, code, models, or tools can be accessed. You can add multiple items to a catalog asset.

<img src="../images/items.png" style="border: 2px solid black;">

5 - If you're not ready to submit, click *Save Draft* at the bottom of the form. You can return later and resume by clicking *Edit* next to your draft.

<img src="../images/edit.png" style="border: 2px solid black;">

6 - Once all required fields are completed, click *Submit*.

**If you submitted an unlisted asset**

Unlisted assets do not go through an approval process. As soon as your submission is complete, the asset is ready to be added to workspaces and CollabStudios.

**If you submitted a public asset**

After submitting your data registration form, you will receive a confirmation email acknowledging receipt of your submission. 

Our team will carefully review your dataset to ensure it meets the standards and requirements of the NDP catalog. Please be patient as we complete this review process, as it may take some time.

Once the review is complete, you will receive a second email informing you of the outcome, either accepting or rejecting your dataset for inclusion in the catalog. If your dataset is rejected, it is because it did not meet the necessary standards.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding the review process or would like clarification on the status of your submission, please don't hesitate to [contact our team](../contact.md) for assistance.

## Updating your approved requests

If you need to update a digital asset after it has been approved, go to *My Approved Requests* and click *Edit* next to the asset you want to edit.
